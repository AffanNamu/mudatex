# The customer desk

*Customers* in the console lists everyone with an online-store account. It needs the *customers.manage* permission over the **whole
company** (not a single shop), because it shows personal details.

- **Find** a shopper by name, email or phone; filter blocked accounts. A "Trade" tag marks businesses with an approved trade account.
- **Look** at their profile, saved addresses, trade account and order history (open the order in *Online orders* to act on it).
- **Block** an account when you must (fraud, abuse): a reason is required and kept. A blocked shopper is refused at once in the shop
  and cannot order; orders already placed carry on. **Lift the block** to restore access.
- **Download everything held about them** (JSON) when a customer asks for their data. Each download is recorded in the audit log.

Not built: deleting a customer. Invoices, sales and tax records must be kept for years, so removing a person is a decision for your
lawyer (what must be kept, what can be erased). The shop's *Privacy* page should say what is kept and for how long.
